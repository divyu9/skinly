⚠️ Protect yourself from courier scams
Parcels can be opened, swapped or emptied on the way. These steps are for your safety — they are the only way a courier will accept a claim, and the only way we can get your money or item back for you.
1. DO NOT ACCEPT a different or opened parcel
Check it at the door. If the packing looks different from the video, or the parcel looks opened, torn or tampered with, refuse it and hand it back to the courier.
2. Record ONE complete, UNCUT unboxing video
Start recording before you open it. Show the sealed parcel from all sides, with the shipping label clearly visible — your name and the AWB number. Then open it on the same video and check every item.
3. Never cut, pause or edit the video
Couriers reject claims made with a cut or edited video. With one uncut video, if anything is missing or different, we fight the claim with the courier for you.
Without this uncut unboxing video, NO claim for a missing, wrong or damaged item can be accepted — in any situation.
